Famara Animation Background

Beautiful 3D Lanzarote landscape as your website background

About This Demo

This page demonstrates how to embed the Famara Animation as a background for your website. The 3D landscape runs behind your content, creating an immersive and engaging user experience.

The animation features a low-poly representation of Lanzarote's landscape with ultra-low LOD buildings for optimal performance.

Key Features

  • Fixed position 3D canvas as background
  • Smooth camera animation through the landscape
  • Ultra-low poly models for optimal performance
  • Responsive design that works on all devices
  • Semi-transparent content cards with blur effects
  • Interactive hover effects on content sections

How It Works

The background canvas uses position: fixed with z-index: -1 to stay behind all content. The content sections have semi-transparent backgrounds with backdrop-filter: blur() for a modern glassmorphism effect.

The 3D animation is built with Three.js and runs continuously in the background, while your website content remains fully interactive in the foreground.
